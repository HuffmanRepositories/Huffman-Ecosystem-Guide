# Privacy and Data Handling

Use synthetic or properly authorized data in examples and tests. Do not commit personal data, credentials, private keys, confidential records, or regulated data.

Before collecting or processing data:
- establish purpose, lawful basis/authority, access controls, minimization, retention, and deletion;
- review applicable privacy and breach-notification obligations;
- avoid sending sensitive data to external AI services without authorization;
- redact logs and issue reports;
- document data flows and third-party processors.

This document is general guidance, not a complete privacy program.
