# Colorado and Denver Legal Considerations

**Status:** Informational starting point only; not legal advice. The laws that apply depend on the system, parties, data, deployment, and use case. A maintainer's location does not make Colorado law the only applicable law.

Before deploying or materially changing an AI-enabled system, consult qualified counsel about applicable federal, state, local, contractual, and international requirements. Consider privacy, consumer protection, discrimination, accessibility, employment, housing, healthcare, financial services, public-sector procurement, and automated decision-making rules as relevant.
