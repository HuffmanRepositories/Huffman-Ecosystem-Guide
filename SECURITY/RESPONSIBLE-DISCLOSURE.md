# Responsible Disclosure

This document is a draft process. Publish a verified contact/channel and response expectations before claiming an active vulnerability disclosure program.

Reporters should:
- provide details privately;
- minimize data access and impact;
- avoid public disclosure before coordinated resolution;
- respect scope and applicable law.

Maintainers should acknowledge, triage, remediate, coordinate disclosure, and document lessons. Do not promise bug bounty payments or legal safe harbor unless formally established.
